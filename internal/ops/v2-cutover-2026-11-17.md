## Learned (folded into the runbook)

- Step 0 readiness shows `"v2":{"status":"off"}` before the flip: the flag is off, expected.
- Step 2: a `book_quality_jobs` row in status 2 is a finished review, not running; `codegen-poller` crash-loops
  and is harmless.
- `etl_reader` password: generated on the server inside one ssh session, never printed.
- Dropping `etl_reader` on the now read-only legacy database needs `BEGIN READ WRITE` around `DROP OWNED BY`.
